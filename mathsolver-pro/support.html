Support

Questions, a wrong step, or an idea? Email us at contact.omarabidi@gmail.com and include the equation you entered and your iPhone model. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

The camera didn't read my equation. Use good light, hold the phone flat above the page, and frame one equation at a time. You can always edit the text before solving.

The AI explanation didn't load. AI explanations need an internet connection. The step-by-step solution itself works offline.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings in the app and tap “Ad Privacy Choices” (shown where required by law), or go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
