Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

MathSolver Pro (“the app”) is developed by the MathSolver Pro team at Telkom University. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We do not require an account and we do not sell your personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your solving history, game scores, leaderboard entries and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera

When you scan an equation, the photo is processed on your device with Apple’s Vision framework to read the text. Photos are not uploaded or saved by the app.

AI explanations (Google Gemini)

When you request an AI explanation, the equation text and its solution steps are sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate the explanation. No name, contact details or device identifiers are included. Google processes this data under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice anytime in the app’s Settings under “Ad Privacy Choices”. On iOS you can also turn off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

We do not operate our own servers and keep no copy of your data. Data sent to Google is handled under Google’s retention policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
